### Deployment Decision Report

This is a **CONDITIONAL GO** for deployment.

Both models, `apertus-8b-instruct-2509-fp8-dynamic` and `nvidia-nemotron-3-nano-30b-a3b-fp8`, are individually deployable to the single GPU, but they **cannot be run concurrently**.
